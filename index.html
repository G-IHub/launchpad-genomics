6-week program structure

Week by week.

W1

Week 1 — Foundation

Tools, data types, and real-world context

Everyone starts together. Introduction to FASTA, FASTQ, BLAST, and how bioinformatics work actually looks. First practical tasks assigned immediately.

W2

Week 2 — Skill Exposure

Analysis, writing, data, and teaching tasks

You try all four types of work. This is how your track is identified — not by what you say you want, but by what you naturally do well.

W3

Week 3 — Track Assignment

You are placed into your specialisation track

Track leads assign you based on Week 1–2 performance. Focused, track-specific training begins. Real tasks, real outputs, real standards.

W4

Week 4 — Applied Work

Track-specific deep training and mini-projects

Independent work begins. You produce your first portfolio-grade output — an analysis report, a research write-up, a cleaned dataset, or a teaching session.

W5

Week 5 — Contract Simulation

Real brief. Real deliverable. Real standards.

You receive a contract-style task brief and must deliver a professional output. This is where OmicsPath verifiers see what you can do.

W6

Week 6 — Evaluation + Selection

OmicsPath verification. Top performers selected.

Final project submitted. OmicsPath assessment determines your layer. Top performers are contacted for paid contracts. Everyone receives certificate and talent profile.
